Shaheed Bhagat Singh

"Inquilab Zindabad"

About Him

Shaheed Bhagat Singh was a great freedom fighter of India. He was born on 28 September 1907 in Punjab. He fought bravely against British rule to make India free.

"They can kill me, but they cannot kill my ideas."

Key Points

  • He started the Naujawan Bharat Sabha for youth.
  • He threw leaflets and non-lethal bombs in the Central Assembly to wake up the British government.
  • He went on a 116-day hunger strike in jail for Indian prisoners.

His Sacrifice

He was hanged on 23 March 1931 with Rajguru and Sukhdev. He was only 23 years old when he sacrificed his life for the country.
